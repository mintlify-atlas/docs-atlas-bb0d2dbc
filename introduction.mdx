AntennaPod is a free and open-source podcast manager for Android that puts you in complete control of your listening experience. Whether you want to subscribe to your favorite shows, download episodes for offline listening, or discover new podcasts, AntennaPod handles it all — without ads, trackers, or subscription fees.

## Get AntennaPod

AntennaPod is available for free on two platforms:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your app store">
    Download AntennaPod from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.danoeh.antennapod) for the full-featured version including Chromecast support, or from [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/app/de.danoeh.antennapod) for a fully open-source build without any proprietary dependencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first podcast">
    Open AntennaPod and tap the **+** button to search for podcasts by name, browse featured directories, or paste a feed URL directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start listening">
    Tap any episode to stream it immediately, or download it for offline playback. Use the queue to line up episodes back-to-back.
  </Step>
</Steps>
