---
title: "Episode queue and inbox: organize your podcast listening"
description: "Use the AntennaPod queue to line up episodes back-to-back, manage the inbox for new arrivals, drag to reorder, and automate what gets queued."
---

AntennaPod separates the job of surfacing new episodes (the **Inbox**) from the job of deciding what plays next (the **Queue**). Understanding both helps you build a listening workflow that fits how you consume podcasts.

## The episode queue

The queue is an ordered list of episodes that play back-to-back. When one episode finishes, the next one in the queue starts automatically. Think of it as a playlist you control.

Open the **Queue** screen from the bottom navigation bar or the side drawer.

### Adding episodes to the queue

You can add episodes to the queue from any episode list — the feed view, the inbox, the all-episodes screen, or the downloads screen.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Single episode">
    Long-press an episode to open the context menu, then choose **Add to queue**. The episode is appended to the end of the queue.

    Alternatively, swipe left or right on an episode (depending on your swipe action settings) to enqueue it immediately.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Multiple episodes">
    Tap and hold an episode to enter multi-select mode, then tap additional episodes to select them. Use the floating action menu at the bottom to **Add to queue**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Removing episodes from the queue

- Long-press an episode in the queue and select **Remove from queue**.
- Swipe the episode left or right (depending on your configured swipe action) to remove it.
- Tap **⋮** → **Clear queue** to remove all episodes at once. A confirmation dialog appears before the queue is cleared.

<Warning>
  Clearing the queue only removes episodes from the queue. It does not delete downloaded files.
</Warning>

### Drag-to-reorder

You can drag episodes to any position in the queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unlock the queue">
    If the queue is locked (the lock icon in the toolbar is active), tap **⋮** → **Lock queue** to toggle it off. Locking prevents accidental reordering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag an episode">
    Touch and hold the drag handle on the right side of any episode row, then drag it up or down to the desired position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to top or bottom">
    Long-press an episode and choose **Move to top** or **Move to bottom** from the context menu as a quick alternative to dragging.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Queue sorting

You can apply a sort order to the entire queue from **⋮** → **Sort queue**. Available sort orders include:

- Date added (oldest or newest first)
- Episode duration (shortest or longest first)
- Feed name (A–Z or Z–A)
- Random shuffle

<Note>
  When **Keep sorted** is enabled in queue settings, the queue is automatically re-sorted when new episodes are added, and the drag-to-reorder handle is hidden. The queue lock option is also hidden while Keep sorted is active.
</Note>

### Queue lock

Enable **⋮** → **Lock queue** to prevent any reordering of episodes. The lock is useful when you want to listen in a fixed order without accidental swipes or drags changing the sequence. A confirmation dialog warns you the first time you enable the lock.

## The inbox

The inbox is a filtered view that shows only **new** episodes — episodes that have arrived since your last feed refresh and have not yet been marked as played or deliberately dismissed.

Open **Inbox** from the bottom navigation bar or the side drawer.

### How episodes arrive in the inbox

Each feed's **New episode action** setting controls whether new episodes go to the inbox, are added directly to the queue, or are ignored.

| Setting | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| **Use global default** | Inherits the app-wide new episodes setting |
| **Add to inbox** | New episodes appear in the inbox |
| **Add to queue** | New episodes skip the inbox and go directly to the queue |
| **Do nothing** | New episodes are fetched but not surfaced automatically |

Configure this per feed under **Feed settings → New episode action**, or globally under **Settings → Inbox**.

### Working with inbox episodes

- **Swipe** an episode to mark it as played and remove it from the inbox.
- **Long-press** to access the full context menu (add to queue, download, mark as played, etc.).
- Tap **⋮** → **Remove all from inbox** to clear every episode from the inbox at once.

### Sorting the inbox

Tap **⋮** → **Sort** to choose the inbox sort order. The inbox supports sorting by:

- Date (oldest or newest first)
- Duration (shortest or longest first)

## Auto-queue behavior

AntennaPod can add episodes to the queue automatically when certain conditions are met.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="New episode action: add to queue" icon="list-plus">
    Set a feed's **New episode action** to **Add to queue**. Every time the feed refreshes and new episodes are found, they are appended to the queue without appearing in the inbox first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Global default" icon="sliders">
    Set a global default under **Settings → Inbox → New episode default action** so all feeds behave consistently unless individually overridden.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If you want a "fire and forget" setup where episodes queue up automatically as soon as they publish, set **New episode action** to **Add to queue** and enable **Auto-download** on the feeds you care about most. New episodes will be downloaded and queued ready to play.
</Tip>
