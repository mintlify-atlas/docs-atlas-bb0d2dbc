---
title: "Audio playback controls and player features in AntennaPod"
description: "Play, pause, seek, and skip episodes in AntennaPod. Use variable speed, sleep timer, chapters, transcripts, and Chromecast from the audio player."
---

AntennaPod's audio player gives you precise control over every aspect of your listening experience — from fine-grained seeking to casting audio to your TV. Open the player by tapping the mini-player bar at the bottom of any screen or by tapping an episode and selecting **Play**.

## Player controls

The player bar shows the core transport controls. These are available both in the full player and from the lock screen or notification shade.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Play / Pause" icon="circle-play">
    Tap the play button to start or resume playback. Tap again to pause. The button state updates in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rewind" icon="backward">
    Jump back by the configured number of seconds (default: 10 s). Long-press the button to change the rewind duration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fast-forward" icon="forward">
    Skip ahead by the configured number of seconds (default: 30 s). Long-press to adjust the fast-forward duration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Skip to next" icon="forward-step">
    Jump to the next episode in the queue. Playback continues automatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Seeking

Drag the progress bar below the playback controls to seek to any position. A floating label shows the current timestamp or chapter title as you drag. Release to confirm the seek.

Tap the elapsed-time label to toggle between **elapsed time** and **time remaining**.

## Variable playback speed

Tap the speed button (showing the current speed such as `1.00`) to open the speed picker. You can choose any speed between 0.5× and 3.0×.

<Tip>
  Each podcast feed can have its own default speed. Go to the feed's settings and set **Playback speed** to override the global default for that show.
</Tip>

Speed changes take effect immediately and the position display automatically converts elapsed and remaining time to match the adjusted speed.

## Sleep timer

The sleep timer stops playback after a set interval so you can fall asleep without missing anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sleep timer">
    In the full player, tap the **⋮** overflow menu and select **Set sleep timer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a duration">
    Pick a duration from the preset list or enter a custom number of minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel or extend">
    When the timer is active, the overflow menu shows **Disable sleep timer**. Tap it to cancel before time runs out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chapter navigation

Episodes that include embedded chapter markers — from both Podcasting 2.0 feeds and ID3 tags in MP3 files — display chapter dividers on the seek bar.

- Chapter boundaries appear as tick marks along the progress bar.
- Drag slowly through the seek bar to snap to individual chapter starts.
- The floating seek label shows the chapter title when you are within a chapter.

<Note>
  Chapters are loaded asynchronously. If chapter marks do not appear immediately, wait a moment for the metadata to finish loading.
</Note>

## Episode transcripts

If a podcast includes a transcript, the **Transcript** option appears in the player overflow menu.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the transcript">
    Tap **⋮** → **Transcript** in the audio player toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow along">
    The transcript dialog displays the full text of the episode. Scroll manually to read ahead or behind the current position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Transcript support is handled by the `:parser:transcript` module and rendered through the `:ui:transcript` module.

## Lock screen and notification controls

AntennaPod registers a media session so you can control playback without unlocking your phone.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Notification" icon="bell">
    The persistent notification shows play/pause, rewind, and fast-forward buttons. Dismiss the notification to stop the player service.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lock screen" icon="lock">
    Album art, episode title, and transport controls appear on the lock screen on Android devices that support media session display.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Bluetooth headset buttons and Android Auto media controls are also routed through the same media session.

## Chromecast support

<Warning>
  Chromecast is only available in the **Google Play** build of AntennaPod. The F-Droid build does not include the Cast SDK because it is a proprietary dependency.
</Warning>

When you are connected to a Chromecast device on the same Wi-Fi network, a cast button appears in the player toolbar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ensure you are on the same network">
    Your phone and the Chromecast must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the cast button">
    The cast button appears in the toolbar of the audio player. Tap it to open the device picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your device">
    Choose your Chromecast from the list. Playback transfers to the device and the player UI switches to cast mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control from your phone">
    All transport controls — play, pause, seek, speed — work while casting. Tap the cast button again to stop casting and return audio to your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Chromecast playback is implemented in the `:playback:cast` module, which implements `PlaybackServiceMediaPlayer` using the Cast SDK and is compiled only in the `play` build flavor.
